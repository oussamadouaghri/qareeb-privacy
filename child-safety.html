Child Safety Standards

Qareeb — قريب منك

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

⚠️ Qareeb is strictly an adults-only application. This app is designed exclusively for users aged 18 years and older. It is a social networking and dating application and is not intended for, nor directed at, children or minors in any way.

1. Age Restriction

Qareeb enforces a minimum age requirement of 18 years. Users must confirm they are at least 18 years old before using the application. We do not permit anyone under the age of 18 to create an account, access features, or interact with other users on our platform.

2. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Policy

Qareeb maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation. Specifically:

  • We strictly prohibit the creation, upload, distribution, or sharing of any content that sexually exploits or endangers children.
  • We prohibit any content that depicts, promotes, or glorifies child abuse in any form.
  • Any user found sharing such content will be immediately and permanently banned from the platform.
  • We will report all instances of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities.

3. Reporting Mechanisms

Qareeb provides multiple ways for users to report child safety concerns:

  • In-App Reporting: Users can report any profile or content that appears to involve a minor or child exploitation through the in-app reporting feature.
  • Email Reporting: Users can report concerns directly to our safety team at contact@qareeb.ma.
  • All reports related to child safety are treated as highest priority and are reviewed promptly.

4. Content Moderation

We employ the following measures to ensure child safety on our platform:

  • User profiles and content are subject to review and moderation.
  • Automated systems and manual reviews help detect and remove prohibited content.
  • Users who violate our child safety policies face immediate account termination.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations related to child safety.

5. Compliance with Laws

Qareeb complies with all applicable laws and regulations regarding child safety, including but not limited to:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The PROTECT Act
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provisions for minors
  • Google Play's Families Policy and Child Safety Standards
  • All applicable regional and national child protection laws

6. Cooperation with Authorities

Qareeb is committed to working with law enforcement and child protection organizations. We will:

  • Report any suspected child exploitation to the appropriate authorities.
  • Preserve and provide relevant data when legally required.
  • Cooperate fully with any investigation related to child safety.

7. Contact Us

If you have any concerns about child safety on our platform, or wish to report a child safety issue, please contact us immediately:

📧 contact@qareeb.ma

All child safety reports are treated with the highest urgency.
