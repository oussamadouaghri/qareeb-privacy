Privacy Policy

Qareeb — قريب منك

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Qareeb ("قريب منك") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Location Data: We access your device's location (with your permission) to show nearby users on the radar map. Location data is processed locally on your device and is not stored on external servers.
  • Profile Information: Any profile details you voluntarily provide within the app (display name, preferences) are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage analytics through Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app and to improve our services.
  • Advertising Data: Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core functionality of the app (radar, messaging, matching)
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze app performance and improve user experience via Firebase Analytics
  • To ensure the security and proper functioning of the app

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Used for anonymous usage analytics. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play Services: Used for location services and core app functionality.

4. Data Storage & Security

All personal data (profile information, chat messages, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate external servers that store your personal data. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Children's Privacy

Qareeb is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about what data is collected
  • Delete: Clear all local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage
  • Opt-out: Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings
  • Ad Preferences: Manage ad personalization through your Google account settings

7. Permissions We Request

  • Location (Fine & Coarse): Required for the radar feature to find nearby users
  • Internet: Required for ads, analytics, and AI chat features
  • Notifications: Optional, used to alert you of new matches and messages

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
